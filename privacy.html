Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Allison Agent is a privately operated desktop application used to create and publish promotional music content for the Allison Interactive artist project.

Information used

When connected to TikTok, Allison Agent may access the account information and authorization data necessary to authenticate the authorized TikTok account and use enabled TikTok content-publishing features.

How information is used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to operate the authorized integration, including authentication, publishing authorized content, and recording the status of publishing operations.

Storage and sharing

Authentication credentials and access tokens are stored locally on the operator's controlled device using secure operating-system facilities where applicable. Allison Agent does not sell personal information or provide TikTok account data to data brokers or advertisers.

Data retention

Authorization data is retained only while required to operate the integration. Local operational records may contain publishing identifiers, timestamps, and content history.

Revoking access

Access may be revoked through the connected TikTok account. Local authorization credentials may also be removed by the operator.

Contact

Questions concerning this policy may be raised through the Allison Agent project repository associated with this site.
